# Availability decision module

`build_availability(records, baseline_dates, target=0.10)` consumes validated weekday rush snapshots from `app.data_loader`. It returns AM and PM sections with latest-day `stations`, station `charts`, and warnings. Dates are ISO strings. The latest day is selected globally across both windows; a station without a snapshot that day gets a dated `no data` dispatch row, while no zero-valued chart point is plotted.

For each station, weekday, and half-open rush window (AM 07:00–09:00; PM 16:00–18:00), `n` counts observed snapshots and `d` counts snapshots with zero available bikes **or** zero available docks. `empty_count` and `full_count` remain separate, so a snapshot that is both empty and full increments each descriptive count but only one `d`. The shared `app.qc.p_chart` computes exact binomial limits separately for AM and PM. The caller passes the original ten baseline dates; a station/window needs observations on all ten for limits. A zero or one baseline share also yields null limits and an explicit insufficient-basis warning. The 0.10 target is an operational threshold, separate from control limits. Signals are exploratory because consecutive five-minute snapshots are dependent.

For each direction, first-unusable time is the median of the first affected snapshot's clock time on affected baseline days. A half-minute median is floored to minute precision for the displayed arrival deadline. The denominator is baseline days with at least one observed snapshot for that station/window, including partially observed days. `onset.empty` and `onset.full` expose `time`, `affected_days`, `observed_days`, `missing_days`, `partial_days`, `observed_snapshots`, `expected_snapshots`, and a label such as `07:40 on 3 of 8 observed days`. The expected count is 24 per baseline window. Missing days or partial days can hide or delay onset, so either case produces a coverage warning.

Latest-day actions use first match: (1) full with disabled bikes: `collect broken bikes first`; (2) above UCL: `investigate: special cause`, with a van direction when empty and full counts differ; (3) in control and baseline share above target: `capacity review: unusable every day`; (4) a larger empty count: `van: drop bikes before HH:MM`, or larger full count: `van: pull bikes before HH:MM`; (5) `OK`. When no baseline onset exists, the van direction has no deadline. Rows with actions come first; station ID resolves ties. A missing current subgroup has `n=0`, null share and limits, and `no data` action.

In synthetic replay, the caller passes only rows through the requested local clock time and the original ten baseline dates. Calibration remains hidden until the tenth baseline PM window closes at 18:00. A current AM or PM window before its 09:00 or 18:00 end reports measured counts and share as provisional, sets limits and flags to null/false, and says `provisional: rush window incomplete` instead of issuing a final dispatch verdict. Each point reports actual `observed_snapshots` versus 24 expected snapshots. A missing current date remains visible in the dispatch table as `no data`; no missing observation is invented or plotted at zero. Onset uses only observed baseline days, with `missing_days` and a coverage warning; partial days count as observed and can delay a first-onset estimate.
